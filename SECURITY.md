# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **[security@openmrp.ai](mailto:security@openmrp.ai)**. Please do not open a public issue, and do not disclose the
vulnerability publicly until we have shipped a fix.

Include whatever you have: affected endpoint or component, the steps to reproduce, what an attacker
gains, and any proof of concept. A working reproduction gets a fix out considerably faster than a
description.

We aim to acknowledge within 3 business days and to give you an assessment and a rough timeline  
within 10. We will keep you updated as the fix progresses and will credit you when it ships, unless  
you would rather stay anonymous.
